About

One person.
Every decision considered.

Akkaris is a one-person independent studio based in Kenitra, Morocco. I'm a software engineer by trade with a genuine interest in making games and apps.

I build games with a focus on tight mechanics, satisfying feedback, and respect for the player's time — and apps with a focus on doing one thing well, without accounts, ads, or your data leaving your device.

Every project starts with a single idea that feels right — and then gets built carefully around that feeling until it's ready to ship.
